# Who we sell to

We sell **white-label QA and test automation** to software development agencies: their clients get the tests, the
agency keeps the relationship and the margin.

## Best fit

- Custom software, web or mobile app development is the main service (at least half of what they do)
- 10–249 employees: big enough to run several client projects at once, too small for an in-house QA team
- Hourly rate of $50/hr or more, so adding QA at our price still leaves them a margin
- Minimum project size of $25,000 or more (long builds that need regression testing)
- Based in the US

## Poor fit

- Marketing, branding, design-only or SEO agencies
- Staffing firms and IT consultancies that place contractors rather than deliver projects
- Offshore shops at $25–49/hr that compete on price (they usually have cheap in-house QA)
- 1,000+ employees (they have QA departments)
